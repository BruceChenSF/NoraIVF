LIRE/SPFresh maintenance-time breakdown on seven streaming workloads. Protocol: full-coverage streams (all vectors are inserted). L=5 is the SPFresh operating point used in our main experiments. *DEEP-9.9M is marked because the Rebuild baseline is absent in Table 5. (a) 100% stacked split of SPFresh maintenance between split clustering and boundary reassignment; boundary reassignment accounts for >99% of LIRE maintenance time on every dataset, so the near-invisible clustering sliver is annotated with its absolute time and share. (b) Same costs on a log y-axis, grouped per dataset: SPFresh split clustering (grey, hatched), SPFresh boundary reassignment (blue) and NoraIVF total maintenance (green); open circles mark the SPFresh-L0 clustering time. L=0 disables reassignment, and its clustering total differs from the main run because the number of splits varies with the stream dynamics. The hatched clustering bars remain visible even where the split cost is a fraction of a second on the log scale.
