QPS-recall frontiers on seven streaming datasets (4+3 panel grid, one panel per dataset; the three bottom panels are horizontally centred). DEEP-9.9M uses the full-coverage rerun (coverage_fraction=1.0, full GT scope, nlist=4096): its QPS is markedly lower than the earlier bounded-window measurement (e.g. Frozen QPS@8=18.2, NoraIVF QPS@8=27.9), so every panel keeps an independent y-axis range instead of a shared QPS scale. Rebuild is absent on DEEP-9.9M (its full-coverage cost would extrapolate to approximately 77 days, so the run was omitted), leaving the DEEP panel with five curves; the shared legend still lists the six methods used by the other six datasets (Frozen, Rebuild, DeDrift, SPFresh, NoraIVF, Ada-IVF).
