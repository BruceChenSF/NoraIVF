Tail-latency CDFs of the mixed streaming workload for seven datasets (top: delete, bottom: query; one CDF per method: Frozen, DeDrift, SPFresh, NoraIVF, Ada-IVF). Each panel uses an independent log-x axis truncated at its pooled 99.9th percentile, so the CDF bulk stays legible while the multi-decade tail is clipped at the right edge. DEEP-9.9M*: latencies were measured over a 200k-operation measurable window with the mixed phase complete (per-operation metering of all 4.99M stream operations would take 70-100 h for Frozen at the 5M live-vector scale); the other six datasets use the full stream.
